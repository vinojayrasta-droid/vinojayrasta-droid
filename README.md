## 📊 Featured Projects

### [Customer Segmentation with RFM Analysis (Excel + Power BI)](https://github.com/vinojayrasta-droid/rfm-customer-segmentation-superstore)

Segmented 793 Superstore customers into 11 behavioral groups using an RFM model built in Excel (quintile thresholds, 125 RFM combinations mapped to segments), then visualized the results in a Power BI dashboard with a recommended marketing action for each segment.

**Key insight:** The At Risk segment holds the largest share of revenue (20.4%), while Champions are only 9.3% of customers but generate 18.2% of sales. Orders with 2 or more sub-categories are worth 2.9x more, which supports a bundling strategy.

### [Sales Funnel: SQL Cleaning & Power BI Dashboard](https://github.com/vinojayrasta-droid/Sales_funnel_dataset)

End-to-end project on a synthetic sales funnel dataset (~1,000 leads) for a heavy equipment rental business. Cleaned messy raw data in PostgreSQL (standardized categories, removed duplicates and invalid dates, converted data types), then built a Power BI dashboard with custom DAX measures to analyze conversion across Lead, Quotation, Negotiation and Closed Won/Lost.

**Key insight:** The leading cause of lost deals is "No response / went cold", well ahead of price or competitors. This points to a follow-up process gap rather than a pricing issue.

### [Funnel and Cohort Analysis (Power BI)](https://github.com/vinojayrasta-droid/funnel-cohort-analysis)

Combined a conversion funnel (education startup data) with a monthly cohort retention analysis (fintech transaction data) to show why acquisition and retention must be tracked together.

**Key insight:** The largest absolute drop-off is from Leads to Bookings (212,938 users, 33.8%). The January 2024 cohort retains best (59.13% still active in month 11), while the February 2024 cohort falls to 26.67% one month after joining.

### [E-commerce Exploratory Data Analysis (Python)](https://github.com/vinojayrasta-droid/ecommerce_eda_analysis)

Exploratory analysis of an e-commerce transaction dataset to answer 5 business questions, from top-performing countries and products to monthly sales trends and the busiest transaction hours. Includes feature engineering, visualization, and multivariate analysis (correlation heatmap).

**Key insight:** Revenue is dominated by 1 country (25x higher than the runner-up) and 2 flagship products, which points to recommendations on stock strategy and market expansion.

### [E-commerce Data Cleaning & Analysis (SQL)](https://github.com/vinojayrasta-droid/ecommerce_sql_analysis)

Practice in data cleaning, feature engineering, and analysis with SQL (PostgreSQL), covering data standardization, derived columns, aggregation, subqueries, and window functions (`RANK() OVER PARTITION BY`) for product ranking per category.

**Key skill demonstrated:** Advanced SQL for cleaning raw data and answering business questions directly from the database.
